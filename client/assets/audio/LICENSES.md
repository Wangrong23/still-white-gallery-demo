use the publicly provided HQ previews, **not** the account-gated lossless originals.

conversion to WAV does not restore information lost in the source MP3. Edits are
reproducible with `scripts/prepare-human-audio.js` and the above source downloads.

## New procedural sound design

Room-tone filtering, fallback piano/bass bed, suspicion/late-day drone, 96 BPM pulse,
heartbeat fallback, tinnitus, breathing/gasp fallback, door mechanism, preparation chimes,
time-penalty mechanism, light relays and siren fallback are generated by project code.
No downloaded third-party samples are embedded in those voices. They retain the
project's licensing and are demo placeholders, not an original OST asset pack.
Normal breath, exhaustion gasp, heartbeat and support siren now use the imported samples
above by default. Their procedural versions only cover missing/undecodable resources.
